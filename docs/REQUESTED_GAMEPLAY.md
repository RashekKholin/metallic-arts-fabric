# Requested gameplay fixes in 1.0.0-rc.2

These eleven requests are implemented. Validation is recorded separately in RELEASE_VALIDATION.md; the precise resource/visibility limits below are intentional.

| Request | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Automatically select thrown coins | Tossing with C while paid Iron/Steel is active selects the actual projectile. Impact transfers its lock to the one recovered item. The newest throw takes priority within the eight-lock budget. Aluminum coins remain immune. |
| Resolve simultaneous Iron and Steel | R explicitly pushes with Steel; V explicitly pulls with Iron. Burning both does not choose an action. Holding both keys applies the signed difference of paid strengths once: equal strength cancels. Both metals still pay normal burn costs. |
| Weaken force at distance | After the ordinary mass/force cap, impulse is multiplied by `1 / (1 + (distance / 8)^2)`. Nearer sources give stronger impulse, with reciprocal recoil and speed caps retained. Multiple selections share one force budget. |
| Full spherical bubbles affecting everyone | Surfaces are latitude/meridian spheres, with continuous client outlines and particle accents. Caster and other players inside receive local movement, jump/fall, attack, mining and item-use rates. Creatures/projectiles and supported machines use local clocks. Leaving restores outside rates; a caster leaving ends that field. |
| Keep out-of-range selection ten seconds | Locks persist for 200 server ticks outside range. No force or fresh remote endpoint is supplied while unavailable. Re-entry before expiry restores the lock. Lifecycle shutdown/explicit Clear still clears locks. |
| All physical metal except aluminum/alloys | Standard materials, ores, equipment, tools, fixtures, metal golems, minecarts, falling metal, tridents and displayed metal are detected. Charged/keyed metal is no longer categorically immune to force. Aluminum, Duralumin and aluminum-containing compositions are excluded. Hidden inventories/internal reserves are not scanned. |
| Connected sources act as one | Face-connected loaded metal blocks share a source key, one line and one selected force-budget entry, even when different metals touch. Equipment attached to one living body is one body target. |
| No stacks above 64 | Portions/flakes/slag, all other registered items and workstation slots cap at 64 or their smaller limit. A 100-portion batch becomes 64+36. Legacy oversized player stacks split on join; workstation overflow is preserved as physical drops. Full inventories do not silently erase surplus. |
| Blue lines for all nearby metal | Owner-only physical endpoints are discovered around the player rather than only under the crosshair. Blue rays point to metal; cyan marks selections. Off-screen/rear markers stay at the screen edge. Source discovery never generates chunks or reveals private ownership/charge. |
| Better in-game controls | Press I or click Arts help above the survival inventory. Three wrapped, scrollable/narrated pages show current rebound keys, targeting, simultaneous forces, storing/tapping and field behavior. The world HUD also shows force/select/coin/help keys while Iron/Steel burns. |
| Bendalloy hunger refill/drain | Filling drains visible food before saturation, keeping at least one food point. Tapping returns paid nutrition to the visible hunger bar. Fractional leftovers stay in persistent body escrow rather than disappearing or creating charge. Higher rates act faster. |

## Explicit scope and accounting

The metal sight radius and nearest-source count are controlled by the existing server detection settings (default 24 blocks / 48 sources, at most 64). Selected and aimed sources are prioritized. Connected-group exploration is loaded-only and bounded to 8,192 metal blocks per group; unloaded boundaries and enormous networks are not an unbounded whole-world graph. Unknown third-party material definitions need `metallicarts:external_metals` / `metallicarts:metal_anchors` tags, and aluminum-alloy exceptions can use the corresponding `pushpull_immune` tags. These limits bound scans and network cost.

Bubbles apply the listed player-local clocks and are real three-dimensional volumes, not ground circles. They do not speed network/keepalive/chat, global physiology, arbitrary redstone, scheduled world ticks or every third-party/vehicle system. Protection and overlap caps remain. Surfaces are public geometry only; payloads contain no owner UUID, private grants or inventories. Sensory preferences can hide visual effects without changing payment or clocks.

One food point remains 400,000 body units. The new exchange converts one ordinary nutrition charge unit into 20 body units, symmetrically for filling and tapping; high-rate tapping retains its extra charge cost. At ordinary tap rate 250 charge units/tick, an empty escrow fills one hunger point in 80 ticks. Partial-food escrow is exact: filling 750 charge units surrenders 15,000 body units, and tapping those same 750 units restores exactly that amount. Existing nutrition-mind charge remains finite but gains this new exchange value; back up worlds before upgrading. No other saved charge or Identity is rewritten.

The full native regression covers distance/recoil, dual input, material immunity, connected/behind discovery, automatic locks and actual impact replacement, 200-tick retention, stack conservation, exact hunger repayment, vertical player sphere entry/exit and real item-use countdowns. Graphical regressions exercise actual owner-only sight packets, inventory help, remapped keys and spherical rendering. Passing tests do not replace the external acceptance tasks in REMAINING_WORK.md.
